# 08a — Real commercial multi-risk policies, batch A (AXA, Allianz, Generali, Groupama, Société Générale/Sogessur)

> Objective: extract **from the actual text of the general conditions** everything a merchant who was looted/vandalised (riots of 17-18 October 2026) can legitimately claim.
> Rule: each row points to the local document + page + short **verbatim** quote. If a clause is not in the document: **« absent »**. Nothing is invented.
> Page convention: « p. X » = number **printed** on the page; « PDF n » = page number of the PDF file when it differs.
> Reminder: the *conditions générales / CG* (general conditions) set the framework. **The actual amounts, options (*valeur à neuf* — replacement-as-new —, *perte d'exploitation / PE* — business interruption —, theft, etc.) and *franchises* (deductibles) are in the client's *conditions / dispositions particulières (CP/DP)* (policy schedule).** The agent must always cross-check CG + CP.

**Honest limitations**
- Allianz: the DG for « Profil Pro Artisan/Commerçant » (the dedicated retail product) are not published online; we analysed **Actif Pro** (same insurer, same architecture, aimed at services/liberal professions). Present it as « contrat Allianz » [an Allianz policy], not as « le contrat des commerçants Allianz » [the Allianz policy for merchants].
- Groupama: only the common core (procedure, deadlines, loss adjustment) is available; **the riot/theft/expenses covers are not in this document → « absent (fascicule non disponible) »** [absent (booklet not available)].

- Generali (January 2022) and Allianz (06/2022) predate the « garantie émeutes » [riot cover] reform discussed in the 2026 press: do not assume they reflect any new statutory regime.

### Traps the agent should detect (evidence in the CG)

1. **Sogessur excludes theft during riots** (p. 19) and theft of goods from the shop window (p. 19).
2. **Allianz Actif Pro: stock capped at €3,000** if the DP say nothing (p. 66); ancillary expenses reserved for fire, storm and water damage (p. 16).
3. **AXA: the « émeutes » [riot] cover excludes theft** (p. 7). Looting is claimed under Theft, and damage under Fire-vandalism. Apportionment is required.
4. **AXA: no BI for mere denial of access due to riots** (p. 29). The shop must have suffered property damage.
5. **Different police-report deadlines**: 24 h with Generali (p. 45) and Allianz (p. 56), 48 h with AXA (p. 54). The notification deadline is 2 business days everywhere. Forfeiture is only enforceable if the insurer proves prejudice.
6. **Assistance (security guard, emergency repairs) excludes riots** with Generali (p. 60) and, as a force majeure case, with Sogessur (p. 56) and Allianz (PDF 42). Security guarding costs must then go through the « frais et pertes » [expenses and losses] cover of the property policy.
7. **Repairs without written consent**: clause « s'abstenir de procéder […] à des réparations sans l'accord préalable » (Generali p. 45, Allianz p. 56). Only protective measures may be carried out without consent.
8. **Current value only if no replacement within 2 years**: the depreciation buy-back is lost if the merchant does not replace or does not keep the invoices.
