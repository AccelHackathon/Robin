# 09b — Denials, appraisal and offers: tactics, countermeasures and adjuster playbooks

> Written 22/10/2026. Complements `09a` (contract traps) and `08d` (process). Here: how a claim is actually fought — the anatomy of a denial, the conduct of the appraisal, the evaluation of an offer, and what public adjusters (US) / loss assessors (UK) concretely do that transposes to the agent.
> Reading rule: every fact is followed by its source. **(unverified)** = working hypothesis. Research synthesis, not legal advice.

---

## 1. Anatomy of a denial: read it as a decision to be taken apart

A denial is not a conclusion, it is a reasoned position to be attacked point by point. The first step is to classify the ground invoked ([Assurance Courtage — contestation method](https://assurancecourtage.fr/refus-indemnisation-assurance-pro-preuves-delais-recours/), [Droit Facile](https://www.droitfacile.fr/assurance-multirisque-professionnelle-voies-de-recours-face-a-un-sinistre-conteste/)):

| Ground invoked | Type of denial | Countermeasure (detail in 09a) |
|---|---|---|
| « Outside the scope of cover » | Non-coverage | Recharacterise the facts; cover clause applicable under the CP (*conditions particulières*, policy schedule) |
| Contractual exclusion | Exclusion | L112-4 (form) + L113-1 « formelle et limitée » [formal and limited] + proof on the insurer |
| Late notification | *Déchéance* (forfeiture of cover) | Clause + proven prejudice + force majeure |
| Breach of prevention obligation | Exclusion/forfeiture | Causal link, proportionality |
| False statement / fraud | Forfeiture/nullity | Bad faith to be proven by the insurer; question never asked |
| Underinsurance | Reduction | Derogating clauses + proof of actual value |
| Deductible / cap / *vétusté* (depreciation) | Reduction | Check the quantified contractual application item by item |
| Unpaid premium | Suspension | *Mise en demeure* (formal notice) + 30-day period respected? |

Substantive rules applicable to every contestation:

- **Burden of proof**: on the policyholder, the occurrence of the loss and its connection to the covered risk; on the insurer, the factual conditions of the exclusion or exoneration it invokes — not reversible by contract (Cass. 2e civ., 2 avr. 1997). Source: [Gdroit](https://gdroit.fr/droit-des-assurances/sinistre-dans-le-cadre-du-contrat-dassurance-la-charge-de-la-preuve/).
- **Quality of evidence is the decisive factor**: the insurance ombudsman notes that photos and a list of damage may prove insufficient — works carried out before the appraisal deprive the expert of the ability to assess the loss. Source: [INC — 2020 mediation report, case 1.11](https://www.inc-conso.fr/content/mediation-de-lassurance-2020-les-principaux-cas-etudies).
- **The denial may be partial**: distinguish « covered loss? », « exclusion applicable? », « amount = actual loss? » — three different fights.
- **Collective precedent (2023 riots)**: France Assureurs had recommended extending the notification deadline to 30 d, expedited appraisals, advance payments, reduced deductibles for small retailers. In a mass event, these sector commitments are a lever to invoke. Source: [France Assureurs press release 6/09/2023](https://presse.tripalio.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/20230906_france_assureurs_cp_violences_urbaines.pdf).

## 2. The battle over deadlines: legal pressure on the insurer

Structural weakness on the policyholder's side: **outside natural catastrophe and motor liability, there is no general statutory settlement deadline** — L113-5 refers to the « délai convenu » [agreed period] in the policy. The annual report of the *Médiation de l'Assurance* (insurance ombudsman) calls this gap a « hole » in ordinary law and recommends regulating handling and decision time. Sources: [08d], [LMA 2023 Report](https://www.mediation-assurance.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Rapport-annuel-2023-LMA_Communique-de-presse.pdf).

What nonetheless exists as pressure:

- **Dated reminders + LRAR** (registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt): every reminder is an exhibit; an LRAR concerning settlement of the claim interrupts the *prescription biennale* (two-year limitation period) (L114-2).
- **Insurer's complaints department**: mandatory step before mediation; the mere act of referring to mediation triggers a re-examination — 36 % of disputes resolved in 2024 were resolved by amicable settlement BEFORE the ombudsman's proposal. Sources: [LMA 2024 Report](https://www.mediation-assurance.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Rapport_annuel_2024_LMA_page-simple.pdf).
- **Late-payment interest**: in natural catastrophe, interest at the statutory rate as of right after 3 months (L125-2 regime); in motor liability, doubling of the statutory rate (L211-13). Outside these regimes: formal notice + insurer's liability for abusive delay (unjustified resistance sanctionable in damages — case law, **to be sourced file by file**).
- **Every deadline missed by the insurer is documented leverage** — see §5 (public adjuster cadence).

## 3. The appraisal: it is won before the site visit

### Mechanics (reminder `08d` + additions)

- Not mandatory outside natural catastrophe/technological/construction-defect regimes; the insurer's decision; amicable settlement below a threshold. Source: [Service-Public F3075](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F3075).
- The policyholder may always appoint their own ***expert d'assuré*** (policyholder's loss assessor); fees at their expense UNLESS the policy includes an « expert fees » cover — typically capped (~5 % of the indemnity, cf. CA Montpellier 30/01/2013). **The presence and cap of this cover is a contractual data point to extract at onboarding.**
- Disagreement → *tiers expert* (umpire) (costs shared); still deadlocked → court-appointed expert via summary proceedings. Appointing an expert interrupts the limitation period. Sources: [France Assureurs](https://www.franceassureurs.fr/lassurance-protege-finance-et-emploie/lassurance-protege/les-demarches-en-cas-de-sinistre/indemnisation-des-dommages-le-role-de-expert-assurance-les-etapes-de-expertise/), [Allianz](https://mesdemarches.allianz.fr/basedoc/je-ne-suis-pas-daccord-avec-lexpertise-de-mon-assurance-que-faire_2249.htm).
- The insurer's expert report **does not impose its conclusions**: the insurer sets the amount; the policyholder contests freely.

### What wins or loses an appraisal

- **Documented problem**: the expert takes notes during the visit; the report arrives later and the policyholder cannot review what was recorded — a source of errors used to deny claims. A counter-signature proposal is under parliamentary debate (written question n° 13211). Source: [AN QE 13211](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/16/questions/QANR5L16QE13211.pdf).
- **Agent countermeasure**: before the visit, build your own file — ***état des pertes*** (statement of loss) quantified item by item, timestamped photos, indexed invoices/inventories, written positions. During the visit: hand the written file to the expert (traceable), take your own notes of statements and measurements. After: send written observations immediately — they enter the file before the report.
- **Prepare the *contre-expertise* (counter-appraisal) as an option, not a reaction**: identify from the opening whether the « expert fees » cover exists and its cap; recommend the policyholder's loss assessor when the economic stakes justify the cost (threshold to be defined — **product decision**).

## 4. The offer: evaluate it, and above all watch what gets signed

### Legal distinctions to detect in every document

- **Advance / provisional payment**: partial payment without waiver — to be cashed/encouraged.
- **Final offer**: the amount can be discussed as long as nothing is signed.
- ***Quittance*** (release/receipt): in itself only a receipt — BUT a *quittance* « pour solde de tout compte » [in full and final settlement] or with « renonciation à toute action » [waiver of any action] amounts to a waiver. The wording prevails over the document's title. Source: [Kohen Avocats](https://kohenavocats.com/offre-indemnisation-assurance-lettre-acceptation-signer-reserves-contester/).
- ***Transaction*** (settlement agreement) (art. 2052 C. civ.): extinguishes any future action on the same subject matter — including for forgotten items. Requires reciprocal concessions: a unilateral waiver by the policyholder without consideration from the insurer was annulled (Cass. 2e civ., 19 oct. 2006 — Carreman case). Sources: [L'Argus — transaction](https://www.argusdelassurance.com/reglementation/legislation/transaction.20946), [Me Dhenry](https://consultation.avocat.fr/blog/elodie-dhenry/article-2969877-proces-verbal-de-transaction-et-offre-d-indemnisation.html).
- **15-day cooling-off exit** (L211-16): reserved for settlements with victims of motor bodily injury — **does NOT exist for the MRP retailer**. A signature is practically final. → Corroborated: [LegalPlanet — release with waiver blocks court-ordered appraisal](https://legalplanet.pro/expertise-provision-refus-renonciation/).

**Golden rule for the agent**: any document produced by the insurer containing « quittance », « transaction », « solde de tout compte », « renonciation », « définitif » is detected automatically and goes through the signature gate — explanation of the terms, of the items covered vs. not covered, of what is being given up. Aligned with the existing decision: acceptance belongs to the retailer, and silence is never acceptance.

### Offer evaluation grid (to be produced by the agent)

1. Item-by-item reconciliation with the statement of loss: covered / partial / denied / absent.
2. Parameter check: depreciation applied vs. contractual formula; per-item caps; deductible; proportional rule (and if so, on what value basis).
3. Nature: provisional or final? What document accompanies it? Waiver included?
4. Quantified gaps with the justification for our figures (clause + page + evidence).
5. Retailer's decision: accept / request clarification / contest — with the € gap and the available levers.

## 5. « Public adjuster » playbook (US) — the agent's direct analogue

The public adjuster is the policyholder's paid representative on a first-party claim. What they do, as documented by their own training guides and regulators:

**The complete « proof of loss » package** — the US equivalent of the statement of loss ([Dolphin Claims — POL strategy](https://dolphinclaims.com/training/public-adjusters/proof-of-loss-strategy/)):

- formal declaration (sworn, dated) — critical date because it starts the insurer's statutory clocks;
- detailed scope + item-by-item estimate;
- organised and indexed photos, video;
- independent expert reports (plumber, engineer…), mitigation and expense receipts;
- contents inventory;
- **« reservation of supplement language »** — the claim reserves the right to supplement: FR transposition = always quantify « subject to supplements » and never freeze a statement of loss as final without an explicit decision.

**File discipline** — probably the most directly copiable pattern ([ClaimFileHQ — intake guide](https://claimfilehq.com/resources/public-adjuster-client-intake-guide)):

- every message to/from the insurer archived in the file, with attachments;
- every call noted the same day: who, what was said, what was agreed;
- every request to the insurer dated, with a follow-up date set at sending;
- **the insurer's positions captured word for word, dated, with the contact's name**;
- the insurer's requests to the policyholder logged with their deadlines on the day of receipt;
- regular status updates to the policyholder without them having to ask.

**Every deadline missed by the insurer = leverage** (documented, e.g. statutory deadlines FL §627.70131). In France: no general statutory deadline, but contractual and sector commitments (France Assureurs 2023) and the use of LRAR reminders play the same evidentiary role.

**US appraisal clause ≈ FR adversarial appraisal**: a contractual mechanism to settle a disagreement on amount — each side appoints an expert, an umpire/*tiers expert* decides. The client's decision, advised and documented.

**Remuneration**: typically ~10 % of the settlement; statutory caps in several States (Texas 10 %, Virginia 10 % in a declared disaster). Sources: [TDI Texas](https://www.tdi.texas.gov/tips/public-adjusters.html), [Virginia SCC](https://scc.es.virginia.gov/media/sccvirginiagov-home/consumer-home/insurance/property--casualty/disaster-readiness/brochure---public-adjuster.pdf). → direct benchmark for the « % of indemnity » model.

## 6. Loss assessor (UK) — what creates the value, as measured by the FCA

- The loss assessor is paid by the policyholder (fees not recoverable from the settlement); the loss adjuster works for the insurer. CMCs/assessors are FCA-regulated. Sources: [ABI/CILA guide](https://propertyclaimassist.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/abi-cmc-guidance-2021.pdf), [Financial.org](https://financial.org.uk/insurance/loss-adjuster-vs-loss-assessor/).
- FCA research on SME claims: the perceived value of assessors is highest on ***perte d'exploitation*** (business interruption) — policyholders discover they can claim more than they thought, and need help preparing the evidence. Denials come « as a shock » because policyholders did not know the terms of their policy. Source: [FCA Quadrangle research 2015](https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/research/quadrangle-commercial-insurance-claims-research.pdf).
- Delays: ~15 % of payments reviewed were late. Source: [FCA Camford Sutton file review](https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/research/camford-sutton-commercial-insurance-claims-smes-report.pdf).

→ Product lesson: the promise is not « more money by magic » but (a) **completeness of items** (the retailer under-declares what they do not know is claimable), (b) **clean evidence** (the shock denial comes from unknown terms — extraction at onboarding kills it), (c) **procedural persistence** (reminders, deadlines, escalation) that the retailer has no time to sustain.

| Médiation de l'Assurance (consumers; eligibility of businesses uncertain — cf. `08d`) | 55 % full/partial success (2024); ~7 months in 2023, 48 % handled < 3 months | 36,540 referrals 2024 | [LMA 2024](https://www.mediation-assurance.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Rapport_annuel_2024_LMA_page-simple.pdf), [Ouest-France](https://www.ouest-france.fr/economie/assurance/assurance-les-consommateurs-de-plus-en-plus-nombreux-a-saisir-le-mediateur-962cea96-881e-11f0-86eb-dac26b435301) |

→ Litigation is the wrong path (3 years, 32 %). The win is secured upstream: complete file, solid evidence, dated reminders, precise knowledge of the policy — exactly the zone where an agent beats an overwhelmed retailer, and where it also beats a human adjuster handling 200 files.

## 8. Operational recap → agent functions

1. **Parse the denial** into a classified ground + extract the clause/law invoked + cite the page.
2. **File cadence**: every communication logged and dated, positions verbatim, reminders scheduled at sending, insurer's deadlines tracked and exploited.
3. **« Subject to supplements »** systematically on every figure transmitted.
4. **Appraisal preparation**: written file handed over, own notes, expert-fees cover checked, threshold for recommending a counter-appraisal.
5. **Signature gate**: detection of release/settlement agreement/waiver → retailer's decision made explicit, never an implicit signature.
6. **Ordered escalation**: reminder → LRAR → insurer complaint → mediation (if eligible) → recommendation of lawyer/policyholder's loss assessor; each step justified by the accumulated exhibits.
