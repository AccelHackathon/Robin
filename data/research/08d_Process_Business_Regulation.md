# 08d — Process, business and regulation: commercial multi-risk (MRP) claims after the looting of 17 October 2026

> Written on 21/10/2026 for the Accel AI Innovate Paris pitch of 22/10/2026.
> Reading rule: every fact is followed by its source. **(unverified)** = not confirmed by a source actually read, or a working assumption. **(computed)** = derived by us from sourced figures.

## 0. Context: the trigger of 17 October 2026

- **The facts**: on Saturday 17/10/2026, looting took place on the fringes of the demonstrations against the cost of living, in at least 7 major cities: Paris (République, Halles, Madeleine, Kawasaki dealership set on fire), Lyon, Bordeaux, Lille, Rouen, Nice and Montpellier. Provisional toll: around 400 arrests. No specific aid for shops had been announced that same evening. Source: [France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/news/pillages-du-17-octobre-comment-les-commercants-peuvent-se-faire-indemniser)
- **Scale**: according to Sud Radio, « près de 2 300 » [nearly 2,300] to « près de 3 000 » [nearly 3,000] shops were affected (both figures appear in the article, with no source cited). Cost estimated by the outlet itself at between €500 M and €1 bn. The Minister for Trade, Serge Papin, promised a « mobilisation à Bercy » [mobilisation at Bercy] with banks and insurers. Source: [Sud Radio](https://www.sudradio.fr/sud-radio/violences-du-17-octobre-jusqua-1-milliard-deuros-de-degats). **Figures to be treated as unverified.**
- **2023 precedent (riots of June-July)**:
  - At 2 months: 15,600 claims for €730 M. Business property accounted for **41 % of notifications and 65 % of the cost**.
  - France Assureurs recommended extending the notification deadline to 30 days (instead of 5), speeding up loss assessments, facilitating advances and easing deductibles for small retailers.
  - Source: [France Assureurs press release of 6/09/2023](https://presse.tripalio.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/20230906_france_assureurs_cp_violences_urbaines.pdf)
  - Cost revised in March 2024: **16,400 claims for €793 M**. Source: [Public Sénat / JeChange](https://www.publicsenat.fr/actualites/parlementaire/emeutes-de-2023-un-cout-colossal-estime-a-un-milliard-deuros-selon-le-rapport-du-senat). The Senate estimates the total cost at around €1 bn, part of the damage having gone unreported.
- **Average cost of a business claim in 2023**: 65 % × €730 M / (41 % × 15,600) ≈ **€74 k** **(computed)**.

## 1. The MRP claims process step by step

| # | Step | Rule / mechanics | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Protective measures** (same day) | The policyholder must limit losses: emergency repairs, protection of property. Failing this, the indemnity may be reduced. Whether the costs are covered depends on the policy. Keep the invoices and take photos before any work. | [Assurland](https://www.assurland.com/assurance-habitation/sinistres/mesures-conservatoires/), [SeLoger](https://edito.seloger.com/conseils-d-experts/reglementations/obligations-de-l-assure-cas-de-sinistre-article-21599.html) |
| 2 | ***Dépôt de plainte* (police complaint)** (theft, vandalism) | Generally required for theft. Online pre-complaint is possible, but it must then be signed at a police or gendarmerie station. Ask for the **procès-verbal** (formal record) in addition to the receipt (sources differ on which document is required). | [France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/news/pillages-du-17-octobre-comment-les-commercants-peuvent-se-faire-indemniser), [LegalPlace](https://www.legalplace.fr/contrats/declaration-de-vol-ou-de-cambriolag/), [Luko](https://www.fr.luko.eu/assurance-habitation/sinistre/proces-verbal-depot-de-plainte/) |
| 3 | **Notification to the insurer** | Legal minimum deadlines (art. L113-2): **2 business days for theft**, 5 business days in the general case. They run from knowledge of the loss. The policy may extend them, never shorten them. Late notification leads to *déchéance* (forfeiture) only if the insurer proves a loss (or absent force majeure). Form: any means (in 2023, France Assureurs accepted post, email, telephone and SMS). | [Selectra](https://selectra.info/assurance/comprendre/retard-declaration-sinistre), [France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/news/pillages-du-17-octobre-comment-les-commercants-peuvent-se-faire-indemniser), [France Assureurs 2023 press release](https://presse.tripalio.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/20230906_france_assureurs_cp_violences_urbaines.pdf) |
| 3bis | **Checking the « émeutes » [riot] cover** | Art. L121-8: the insurer does not cover riots and civil commotion, **unless otherwise agreed**. It is for the insurer to prove that the loss results from a riot. Other covers may apply: theft, vandalism, glass breakage, and *perte d'exploitation* (business interruption) if caused by an insured damage. | [France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/news/pillages-du-17-octobre-comment-les-commercants-peuvent-se-faire-indemniser) |
| 3ter | **Claim against the State** (in parallel) | Art. L211-10 of the CSI: the State is civilly liable for damage caused by *attroupements* (unlawful assemblies), with no fault to prove. Procedure: prior claim to the prefect, then administrative court. Four-year limitation period. The insurer that has paid is subrogated. Limit: the Conseil d'État (28/10/2022, n° 451659) excludes premeditated acts by an organised group, which is a risk for the black blocs of 17/10. | [France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/news/pillages-du-17-octobre-comment-les-commercants-peuvent-se-faire-indemniser) |
| 4 | ***État des pertes* (statement of loss)** | Itemised inventory of destroyed or stolen property (building and improvements, equipment, goods), with supporting documents: invoices, inventories, accounting records, delivery notes, stock movements, photos, quotes. Without invoices, the insurer may rely on stock accounting. | [Assurup](https://www.assurup.com/blog/articles/indemnisation-sans-facture-achat), [Pacifica MRE](https://credit-agricole.fr/content/dam/assetsca/master/public/commun/documents/Assurance_Multirisques_Entreprise.pdf). No legal definition of the « état des pertes » found **(unverified)**. |
| 5 | **Insurer's loss assessment** | Art. R112-1: the policy must set out the procedure for estimating damage. Most provide for a negotiated settlement, with a loss adjuster only above a threshold or in case of disagreement. The major loss-adjusting groups are Stelliant, Adenes, Saretec and Alkera (parent of Polyexpert). Texa, Elex and Eurexo are also present. | [Ministerial answer n° 2129, JO 8/01/2019](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/15/questions/QANR5L15QE2129.pdf), [Xerfi](https://www.xerfi.com/presentationetude/le-marche-des-experts-en-assurance-dommages_ABF17), [L'Argus](https://www.argusdelassurance.com/assurance-dommages/expertise/expertise-assurance/4/) |
| 6 | **Adversarial assessment / *contre-expertise* (counter-appraisal)** | « Chaque fois qu'un expert intervient […] l'autre partie doit être en mesure de désigner son propre expert. » [Whenever an expert is involved, the other party must be able to appoint its own expert.] The fees of the policyholder's loss assessor are borne by the policyholder, unless there is « honoraires d'expert » [loss assessor's fees] cover. The insurer's loss adjuster does not impose his conclusions: the insurer remains free to set the amount. | [RM 2129](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/15/questions/QANR5L15QE2129.pdf), [APHP – amicable assessment](https://affairesjuridiques.aphp.fr/textes/lexpertise-amiable-dassurance/), [Luko](https://fr.luko.eu/conseils/guide/expert-assurance/) |
| 7 | ***Tiers expert* (umpire / third expert)** | If the two experts disagree, they bring in a 3rd expert, appointed by the parties or, failing that, by the president of the court (formerly TGI). His fees are generally split equally. | [RM 2129](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/15/questions/QANR5L15QE2129.pdf), [APHP](https://affairesjuridiques.aphp.fr/textes/lexpertise-amiable-dassurance/) |
| 8 | ***Procès-verbal d'expertise* / letter of acceptance** | The amicable agreement is formalised in a ***procès-verbal d'expertise*** (assessment report). The *quittance* (release/receipt) or the acceptance often amounts to a waiver, and a settlement bars any later action with the same object (art. 2052 C. civ.). **Critical point: do not sign too early.** | [Doctrine – TJ 2026](https://www.doctrine.fr/d/TJ/Roanne/2026/TJPAA024205750E39B2DEE2) (snippet only), [Forum Construire](https://www.forumconstruire.com/construire/topic-165981.php) (non-authoritative) **(partially verified)** |
| 9 | **Payment / advances** | For MRP outside natural disasters, **there is no general legal deadline**: art. L113-5, the insurer pays « dans le délai convenu » [within the agreed time] in the policy. A contractual period of 30 days after agreement is common **(unverified)**. For natural disasters (art. L125-2, loi 2021-1837): offer within 1 month after the estimate or final report, then payment within 21 days after agreement. Advances are not a legal obligation: France Assureurs recommended them in 2023. | [Assurup L113-5](https://www.assurup.com/blog/articles/article-l-113-5-code-assurances), [Selectra cat nat](https://selectra.info/assurance/actualites/marche/catastrophe-naturelle-delai-paiement-assureur), [FA 2023 press release](https://presse.tripalio.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/20230906_france_assureurs_cp_violences_urbaines.pdf) |
| 10 | **Deferred *vétusté* (depreciation)** | The *valeur à neuf* (replacement-as-new) top-up is paid only after actual replacement, against invoices, generally within 2 years. Cass. 2e civ., 21/01/2021, n° 19-22.529 (cited, not re-read). | [France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/academy/vetuste-valeur-a-neuf-indemnisation-formule) |
| 11 | **Mediation** | The *Médiation de l'Assurance* (insurance ombudsman) is free and can be referred to 2 months after a written complaint (since 1/01/2023). It issues an opinion within around 90 days. It received 36,537 referrals in 2024 and around 47,000 in 2025. **Caution: its charter targets « consommateurs » [consumers]**, so a merchant with an MRP policy is probably not eligible **(to verify per insurer)**. | [Tout sur mes finances](https://www.toutsurmesfinances.com/actualites/?p=1739), [France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/academy/mediateur-de-l-assurance-saisir-procedure), [Charter (SMACL)](https://www.smacl.fr/files/documents/charte-mediateur-assurance.pdf) |
| 12 | **Two-year limitation period** | Art. L114-1: 2 years from the event. Art. L114-2: the limitation period is interrupted notably by the **appointment of a loss assessor** or by an *LRAR* (registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt) concerning settlement. It is enforceable only if the policy reproduces these rules. | [LegalPlace](https://www.legalplace.fr/guides/prescription-biennale/), [Eurojuris](https://www.eurojuris.fr/articles/jurisprudence-en-matiere-de-construction-procedure-2392.htm) |

**Typical durations**
- There are no public statistics on the duration of an MRP claim.
- Field order of magnitude: a few weeks for a small theft or glass breakage settled by negotiation, several months to over a year for a fire with business interruption and counter-appraisal **(unverified)**.
- Benchmark: Trustpilot reviews of loss-adjusting firms mention recurring complaints about delays ([Trustpilot Saretec](https://fr.trustpilot.com/review/www.saretec.fr?page=3)).

## 2. Calculating the indemnity

- **Indemnity principle (L121-1)**: the indemnity cannot exceed the value of the property on the day of the loss. There is no enrichment. Source: [BJDA](https://bjda.fr/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/VGF78po8_Comm_CA_4.pdf).
- **Actual cash value, replacement-as-new and depreciation buy-back**:
  - Compensation is first paid at actual cash value (replacement-as-new less depreciation).
  - The « valeur à neuf » top-up is paid after reconstruction or replacement. It is **most often capped at 25 %** (sometimes 33 %) and paid within 2 years.
  - Depreciation is not defined by the Code: scales are specific to each insurer.
  - Sources: [France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/academy/vetuste-valeur-a-neuf-indemnisation-formule), [Luko](https://fr.luko.eu/conseils/guide/valeur-usage-ou-a-neuf/). These sources concern home insurance (MRH); transposition to MRP is **(unverified)**.
- **Limits and *franchises* (deductibles)**:
  - Covers are capped per heading (theft, glass breakage, goods…), with a deductible per claim.
  - Ancillary costs are often expressed as a % of the indemnity. MACSF MRP example: ancillary costs at 15 % of the base indemnity, **including a maximum of 5 % for the *expert d'assuré* (policyholder's loss assessor)**.
  - Sources: [MACSF MRP IPID](https://www.macsf.fr/content/download/46248/fichier/0117245_C_DIPA_MRP_MACSF_DATAMATRIX_VF.pdf), [Pacifica](https://credit-agricole.fr/content/dam/assetsca/master/public/commun/documents/Assurance_Multirisques_Entreprise.pdf)
- ***Règle proportionnelle de capitaux* (underinsurance averaging) (L121-5)**:
  - In case of underinsurance, the indemnity equals the damage × (insured value / actual value). It applies to all property insurance, unless otherwise agreed (Cass. 18/06/1996, n° 94-15.105).
  - It is distinct from the proportional **premium** rule (L113-9), which penalises a misrepresentation of the risk.
  - It has been applied to an inaccurately declared *marge brute* (gross margin) in business interruption, despite a waiver clause (Cass., cited by RGA 2017, date unverified).
  - Sources: [Lextenso](https://www.labase-lextenso.fr/jp-commente/cc/1996-06/94-15-105-CC-18061996-94_15105), [Luko](https://fr.luko.eu/conseils/guide/regle-proportionnelle-capitaux/), [RGA 2017](https://static.labase-lextenso.fr/pdf-sommaires-revues/RGA/RGA-2017-04.pdf)
  - **To avoid it**: waiver of the proportional rule clause, agreed value, prior valuation of sums insured, indexation. UPEMEIC publishes revaluation coefficients every year to update sums insured derived from prior valuations ([UPEMEIC](https://upemeic.com/nos-actions/)).
  - Context figure: 65 % of very small businesses and SMEs have cover limits that no longer keep pace with their growth ([Hiscox 2025](https://www.hiscox.fr/courtage/sites/courtage/files/documents/202511-rapport-hiscox-sous-assurance.pdf)).
- ***Perte d'exploitation* (business interruption, PE)**:
  - It applies only if the interruption of activity is due to an **insured material damage**.
  - It covers the loss of **gross margin**, i.e. turnover less variable costs. Permanent overheads (rent, salaries, depreciation, interest) are therefore included. It also covers **additional operating costs** incurred with the insurer's agreement, such as temporary premises or equipment.
  - Basis of calculation: gross margin of the last financial year, adjusted for foreseeable growth, often with a +20 % adjustability.
  - **Indemnity period**: set at inception, at least 12 months for fire or explosion.
  - Deductible in days, in value or in %.
  - « Carence d'accès » [denial of access] extension if access to the premises is impossible because of a loss in their vicinity.
  - Source: [France Assureurs – PE](https://www.franceassureurs.fr/lassurance-protege-finance-et-emploie/lassurance-protege/lassurance-en-pratique-pour-les-professionnels/assurance-des-pertes-exploitation-de-entreprise/)
  - **Practical formula**: loss of turnover × gross margin rate − saved costs + additional costs ([Messika](https://messika.fr/expert-assure-pertes-exploitation/)).
  - **Example**: restaurant with €500 k turnover, 40 % gross margin, 4 months' closure. 40 % × €166.7 k = €66.7 k, plus €15 k of additional costs, i.e. €81.7 k ([BL Assur](https://www.bl-assur.fr/assurance-pertes-dexploitations-que-couvre-t-elle/)).
  - **Lever for the policyholder's loss assessor**: fixed costs wrongly classified as variable costs lower the gross margin rate, and therefore the indemnity ([Messika](https://messika.fr/expert-assure-pertes-exploitation/)).
  - Documents required: balance sheets and *liasses fiscales* (statutory tax filings) for N-1 and N-2, monthly turnover and seasonality, general ledger of expenses **(unverified, standard practice)**.
- **Goods**:
  - The basis depends on the policy: **cost or purchase price** (most common case) or selling price, if a clause provides for it. Selling price includes the lost margin and must therefore be negotiated at inception.
  - Sources: [Companeo](https://www.companeo.com/assurance-de-marchandises/guide/l-assurance-des-marchandises-transportees), [Érudit](https://erudit.org/fr/revue/assurances/1951/v19/n1/1103194ar.pdf)
  - Point of attention: where PE is covered, the margin on lost stock often ends up in the PE (risk of double counting) **(unverified)**.
- **Tenant's improvements**: these are the tenant's fit-out works, insured separately from the building. No quality source was read on how they are valued **(unverified)**.
- **Loss assessors' method**: item-by-item costing, based on quotes, invoices, replacement price of comparable equipment and accounting inventories ([Assurup](https://www.assurup.com/blog/articles/indemnisation-sans-facture-achat)). Simple cases are shifting to video assessment, automation and AI ([Xerfi](https://www.xerfi.com/presentationetude/le-marche-des-experts-en-assurance-dommages_ABF17)).

## 3. The policyholder's loss assessor profession

- **Status**:
  - **Unregulated profession, unprotected title.** « Seule la profession d'expert en automobile relève d'un dispositif législatif et réglementaire. » [Only the motor-vehicle assessor profession is subject to a legislative and regulatory framework.] Policyholders and insurers are free to choose their expert. Source: [RM n° 2129, JO 8/01/2019](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/15/questions/QANR5L15QE2129.pdf)
  - The CNEA is the **Commission nationale des experts en automobile** (national motor-vehicle assessors commission), unrelated to policyholder's loss assessors ([AN QE 46631](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/14/questions/QANR5L14QE46631.pdf)).
- **Professional organisations**:
  - **UPEMEIC** (Union professionnelle des experts en matière d'évaluations industrielles et commerciales), founded in 1947: claims **80 % of the market's headcount**. It created the « UPE2018 Expert d'Assuré Certifié » certification with Bureau Veritas, a code of ethics and a **fee scale** (according to Le Journal des Entreprises, « tous les experts d'assurés ne tiennent pas compte de ce barème » [not all policyholder's loss assessors follow this scale]). Sources: [UPEMEIC](https://upemeic.com/nos-actions/), [L'Argus](https://www.argusdelassurance.com/acteurs/experts-d-assures-une-charte-deontologique-pour-valoriser-la-profession.111798), [JDE](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/article/grace-un-expert-dassures-jai-gagne-30-dindemnisation-2121845)
  - **FEDEXA** (Fédération des experts d'assurés): **27 firms listed**, of which 10 are IFPASS-certified (certification launched in 2021). Source: [FEDEXA](https://www.fedexa.fr/les-experts-fedexa)
  - Regional networks, for example SINAURA (9 firms). Source: [Expertise OPS](https://expertise-ops.com/)
- **Number of players**:
  - Directories list around **104 to 120 establishments** under « expert d'assuré » ([Carnet des Entreprises](https://carnet-des-entreprises.fr/services/expert-d-assure/)), a figure that is probably underestimated.
  - Kompass lists 2,532 companies under « experts en assurances », all categories combined ([Kompass](https://fr.kompass.com/a/experts-en-assurances/82930/)).
  - Working estimate: **a few hundred firms** **(unverified)**.
  - Example of a firm cited by Xerfi: Cabinet Roux ([Xerfi](https://www.xerfi.com/presentationetude/le-marche-des-experts-en-assurance-dommages_ABF17)).
- **Fees**:
  - **5 to 10 % of the indemnity** (or of the uplift obtained) ([France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/academy/assurance-habitation/glossaire-expert-d-assure)).
  - Example of an engagement letter adjudicated in Toulouse in 2025: **5 % excl. VAT of the sums to be received** ([Doctrine TC Toulouse 2025](https://www.doctrine.fr/d/TCOM/Toulouse/2025/TCOMP6E54C35B85AFB174C9C2)).
  - Frequent litigation over the fee base (« totalité des indemnités » [all indemnities] or indemnity actually paid) ([CA Agen, 22/07/2026](https://www.doctrine.fr/d/CA/Agen/2026/CAP160F6D9D65E9A7603036)).
  - A one-off home insurance counter-appraisal costs €500 to €2,000 ([Selectra](https://selectra.info/assurance/assurance-habitation/expert)).
- **Coverage by the policy**:
  - « Honoraires d'expert » cover: for example MACSF MRP, up to a maximum of 5 % ([MACSF IPID](https://www.macsf.fr/content/download/46248/fichier/0117245_C_DIPA_MRP_MACSF_DATAMATRIX_VF.pdf)).
  - Judgments are contradictory on the reimbursement of fees as a head of loss: accepted at the [TJ Versailles in 2024](https://www.doctrine.fr/d/TJ/Versailles/2024/TJPCF5F88A14E0691D6FB93), refused at the [TJ Clermont-Ferrand in 2026](https://www.doctrine.fr/d/TJ/Clermont-Ferrand/2026/TJPDC363E35F1DBEEAC19BE).
- **Value added (testimony)**: the Omnia nightclub, arson. The owner expected around €600 k and obtained around €995 k, including €230 k of PE. « Il y aurait eu 30 % d'écart sans expert d'assurés. » [There would have been a 30 % gap without a policyholder's loss assessor.] Source: [JDE](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/article/grace-un-expert-dassures-jai-gagne-30-dindemnisation-2121845). This is a single case, of anecdotal value.
- **Mandate**: engagement letter or mandate (civil-law mandate). The policyholder's loss assessor defends the interests of the claimant ([France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/academy/assurance-habitation/glossaire-expert-d-assure)).
- **« Consultation juridique » (legal advice as a regulated activity) limit (loi n° 71-1130, art. 54 à 60)**:
  - Art. 54 reserves paid legal advice to the legal professions. Art. 60 allows it on an **ancillary** basis for other professionals, subject to conditions.
  - **Cass. 2e civ., 7/05/2025, n° 23-21.455**: conviction of a « mandataire d'assuré » (policyholder's representative) in personal injury (loi Badinter) who analysed offers, advised on next steps, drafted counter-proposals and charged success fees.
  - The ruling distinguishes what remains outside the scope of legal advice: « une simple gestion administrative du dossier » [mere administrative handling of the file] and « une discussion purement technique aboutissant à un calcul automatique » [a purely technical discussion leading to an automatic calculation].
  - It does not directly address property damage.
  - Sources: [CNB](https://cnb.avocat.fr/actualite/le-cnb-et-l-ordre-des-avocats-de-marseille-obtiennent-la-condamnation-d-un-mandataire-d-assure-devant-la-cour-de-cassation), [Lextenso 2025](https://www.labase-lextenso.fr/l-essentiel-droit-des-assurances/2025-n7/intermediaire-en-assurance-et-consultation-juridique-la-frontiere-reaffirmee-DAS202r5), [Cass. 1re civ. 9/12/2015 broker](https://www.labase-lextenso.fr/gazette-du-palais/2016-n1/le-conseil-en-assurance-donnait-des-consultations-juridiques-reservees-aux-avocats-GPL253c2)
  - **Product consequence**: the agent must position itself on **technical, quantified valuation** and file organisation. Interpretation of disputed clauses and litigation strategy must go through a policyholder's loss assessor or a lawyer.
- **ORIAS / intermediation**:
  - Art. L511-1 (transposing the 2005 IDD): « N'est pas considérée comme de l'intermédiation […] l'activité consistant **exclusivement** en la gestion, l'estimation et la liquidation des sinistres. » [Is not considered intermediation an activity consisting exclusively in the management, estimation and settlement of claims.] Sources: [AN pl2558](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/12/pdf/projets/pl2558.pdf), [Sénat](https://www.senat.fr/leg/pjl04-285.html)
  - A tool that only helps to quantify and manage a claim therefore does not fall under ORIAS.
  - By contrast, recommending or selling a « mieux couverte » [better-covered] MRP policy would tip into intermediation, hence the need for a functional approach ([Victoris](https://www.victorisavocat.com/blog/mandat-intermediaire-assurance-ias-statuts-clauses-sensibles)). The consolidated version on Légifrance has not been re-read **(to verify)**.
- **« Vautours » [vultures] reputational risk**: after the 2023 riots, the Gironde prefecture advised against accepting experts who present themselves unsolicited (search summary; [prefecture PDF](https://www.gironde.gouv.fr/contenu/telechargement/68384/509935/file/Accompagnement%20des%20commerces%20touch%C3%A9s%20par%20les%20%C3%A9meutes%20urtbaines.pdf), not re-read, site under maintenance) **(partially verified)**. **The go-to-market must not rely on door-to-door canvassing.**
- ***Courtiers* (brokers)**: they handle their clients' claims. This activity is attached to the follow-up of the policy placed, hence ancillary (art. 59), according to Cass. 1re civ. 9/12/2015, which a contrario convicts a broker who stepped outside this perimeter ([Lextenso](https://www.labase-lextenso.fr/gazette-du-palais/2016-n12/exercice-illegal-d-une-consultation-juridique-par-un-courtier-d-assurances-GPL260n9)).
- **Industry software**:
  - **DARVA / Sinapps IRD**: exchange platform between insurers, loss adjusters and repairers. More than 140 insurer and broker clients, 16,000 provider sites, around 8 M files in 2022 (motor and property). Source: [Covéa 2023 press release](https://www.covea.com/sites/default/files/2023-11/communique_de_presse_sinapps_auto_flash_un_partenariat_gagnant_pour_covea_et_darva_20231107.pdf), [Auto-Infos](https://www.auto-infos.fr/article/darva-veut-faire-du-dossier-partage-le-nouveau-socle-de-la-gestion-du-sinistre.293507)

- **Potential policyholder's loss assessor fees on retailer MRP**: €1.3 bn of claims × 5 to 10 % fees ≈ **€65 to 130 M/year**. This is a **theoretical ceiling**: it assumes 100 % of files go through a policyholder's loss assessor, which is false for small claims.

## 6. Data and integrations: what is feasible

- **Mandate**:
  - The policyholder may appoint a third party (policyholder's loss assessor) to represent him in the adversarial assessment. This is the core of the profession: « le débat contradictoire entre les experts qui établit l'indemnité finale » [the adversarial debate between the experts that sets the final indemnity] ([RM 2129](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/15/questions/QANR5L15QE2129.pdf)).
  - A **software agent** can therefore prepare, send and track exchanges **on behalf of the policyholder or his appointed loss assessor**.
  - Negotiation with legal content (analysis of offers and legal counter-proposals) is at risk in light of Cass. 7/05/2025 (see section 3).
  - Recommendation: a human in the loop, the policyholder's loss assessor, who signs and holds the mandate.
- **Real channels**:
  - For very small businesses, notification and exchanges go through email, post, telephone and the customer portal, and through the broker or general agent. France Assureurs accepted « tout moyen » [any means] in 2023 ([FA press release](https://presse.tripalio.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/20230906_france_assureurs_cp_violences_urbaines.pdf)).
  - Insurer–loss adjuster exchanges go through DARVA/Sinapps, a network closed to policyholders **(unverified)**.
  - **There is no public insurer API for the policyholder** **(unverified)**: the product must be built on email and documents.
- **Typical documents**:
  - Policy: *conditions particulières* (policy schedule) and *conditions générales* (general conditions).
  - Event documents: notification, police complaint record and receipt, photos and videos.
  - Valuation documents: purchase invoices, inventory and stock movements, delivery notes, repair or replacement quotes.
  - Business interruption documents: balance sheets and statutory tax filings for N-1 and N-2, monthly turnover.
  - Costs: invoices for protective measures.
  - Assessment documents: insurer's loss adjuster report, assessment report, release.
  - Sources: [Assurup](https://www.assurup.com/blog/articles/indemnisation-sans-facture-achat), [France Assureurs PE](https://www.franceassureurs.fr/lassurance-protege-finance-et-emploie/lassurance-protege/lassurance-en-pratique-pour-les-professionnels/assurance-des-pertes-exploitation-de-entreprise/), [Groupama](https://www.groupama.fr/assurance-professionnels/conseils-pro/sinistre-vol)
- **Insurer's loss adjuster report**: whether it is communicated to the policyholder depends on the policy ([Que Choisir](https://www.quechoisir.org/conseils-expertise-amiable-et-expertise-judiciaire-quels-sont-vos-droits-n121418/)). It must therefore be requested explicitly: this is a task the agent can automate.
- **GDPR**: accounting documents and personal data (police complaint record). A DPA and EU hosting are required **(unverified, good practice)**.

## 7. Answers to the 10 questions an Accel partner will ask

1. **« Pourquoi maintenant ? » [Why now?]**
   17/10/2026: 2,300 to 3,000 shops affected according to [Sud Radio](https://www.sudradio.fr/sud-radio/violences-du-17-octobre-jusqua-1-milliard-deuros-de-degats) (unverified). Unlike 2023, no insurer measure had been announced ([France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/news/pillages-du-17-octobre-comment-les-commercants-peuvent-se-faire-indemniser)). On the technology side, the category has just been validated in the US: Finch ($4.5 M, 09/2026) and Tugboat ($3 M, 06/2026).

2. **« Quelle taille de marché ? » [What market size?]**
   Around €1.3 bn/year of MRP claims for tradespeople and retailers (computed: €2.6 bn × 51 % loss ratio, [France Assureurs 2025](https://www.france-epargne.fr/news/locaux-professionnels-les-indices-montent-le-ratio-sinistres-a-primes-au-plus-bas-en-52-ans)), i.e. €65 to 130 M/year of fees at 5–10 %. Across the whole business line (€9.5 bn of premiums), the theoretical pool is €220 to 450 M. On top of this come event-driven peaks: €515 M of business claims in 2023.

3. **« Qui paie ? » [Who pays?]**
   The merchant, on success: 5 to 10 % of the indemnity ([France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/academy/assurance-habitation/glossaire-expert-d-assure); 5 % excl. VAT in a contract [adjudicated in 2025](https://www.doctrine.fr/d/TCOM/Toulouse/2025/TCOMP6E54C35B85AFB174C9C2)). These fees are often partly reimbursed by the MRP's « honoraires d'expert » cover (up to 5 % at [MACSF](https://www.macsf.fr/content/download/46248/fichier/0117245_C_DIPA_MRP_MACSF_DATAMATRIX_VF.pdf)). The net cost for the policyholder can therefore be low.

4. **« Est-ce légal ? Les avocats ne vont-ils pas vous attaquer ? » [Is it legal? Won't the lawyers come after you?]**
   The profession is not regulated ([RM 2129](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/15/questions/QANR5L15QE2129.pdf)). But the Cass. ruling of 7/05/2025 (n° 23-21.455) sanctions paid legal advice by a representative. It leaves « gestion administrative » [administrative handling] and « calcul technique » [technical calculation] outside its scope ([CNB](https://cnb.avocat.fr/actualite/le-cnb-et-l-ordre-des-avocats-de-marseille-obtiennent-la-condamnation-d-un-mandataire-d-assure-devant-la-cour-de-cassation)). We therefore stay on quantification and the file, with an appointed human loss assessor, and a lawyer as relay for litigation.

5. **« Faut-il un agrément ORIAS ? » [Is ORIAS registration required?]**
   No, as long as the activity remains exclusively « la gestion, l'estimation et la liquidation des sinistres » [the management, estimation and settlement of claims], excluded from intermediation by art. L511-1 ([AN pl2558](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/12/pdf/projets/pl2558.pdf)). By contrast, selling or recommending policies would tip into intermediation. Consolidated version to verify.

6. **« Qui sont vos concurrents ? » [Who are your competitors?]**
   In France and Europe, all the claims AI identified is insurer-side: Shift (unicorn), Zelros, Tractable, and the loss-adjusting firms Stelliant, Saretec, Alkera ([Xerfi](https://www.xerfi.com/presentationetude/le-marche-des-experts-en-assurance-dommages_ABF17)). On the policyholder side, there are only fragmented human firms (UPEMEIC, FEDEXA: 27 firms). In the US, Finch and Tugboat are not present in France.

7. **« Pourquoi l'assuré a-t-il besoin de vous ? L'assureur paie déjà. » [Why does the policyholder need you? The insurer already pays.]**
   The loss adjuster is appointed by the insurer, which « reste libre de fixer le montant » [remains free to set the amount] ([APHP](https://affairesjuridiques.aphp.fr/textes/lexpertise-amiable-dassurance/)). The pitfalls are technical: underinsurance averaging (L121-5), depreciation, miscalculated gross margin. Testimony: +30 % indemnity with a policyholder's loss assessor ([JDE](https://www.lejournaldesentreprises.com/article/grace-un-expert-dassures-jai-gagne-30-dindemnisation-2121845)). At Finch, public adjusters' estimates are 50 % above insurers' (company figure).

8. **« Comment accédez-vous aux données ? Les assureurs ont des systèmes fermés. » [How do you access the data? Insurers have closed systems.]**
   The policyholder holds his own documents: policy, invoices, tax filings, photos, police complaint record. Exchanges with the insurer go through email and post ([FA 2023 press release](https://presse.tripalio.fr/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/20230906_france_assureurs_cp_violences_urbaines.pdf)). We do not need an insurer API. DARVA/Sinapps serves insurers and loss adjusters ([Covéa](https://www.covea.com/sites/default/files/2023-11/communique_de_presse_sinapps_auto_flash_un_partenariat_gagnant_pour_covea_et_darva_20231107.pdf)); it is a medium-term avenue.

9. **« Comment acquérez-vous les clients sans faire de démarchage “vautour” ? » [How do you acquire customers without “vulture” canvassing?]**
   After 2023, prefectures warned against unsolicited experts ([Gironde, summary](https://www.gironde.gouv.fr/contenu/telechargement/68384/509935/file/Accompagnement%20des%20commerces%20touch%C3%A9s%20par%20les%20%C3%A9meutes%20urtbaines.pdf), not re-read). Our channels: existing policyholder's loss assessors (SaaS tool and white label), chartered accountants (they hold the tax filings needed to calculate PE), CCIs and retailers' federations, brokers (unverified).

10. **« Quels sont les risques business ? » [What are the business risks?]**
    - **Event-driven seasonality**: in 2025, the retailer MRP loss ratio is the lowest in 52 years ([France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/news/locaux-professionnels-les-indices-montent-le-ratio-sinistres-a-primes-au-plus-bas-en-52-ans)). « Normal » volume is therefore low and peaks are driven by crises (riots, 2026 wildfires: around 3,000 business claims, [Option Finance](https://www.optionfinance.fr/info-financiere-en-continu/d/2026-08-28-incendies-25-000-sinistres-pour-un-cout-estime-a-pres-de-500-millions-deuros.html)).
    - **Fee disputes** ([CA Agen 2026](https://www.doctrine.fr/d/CA/Agen/2026/CAP160F6D9D65E9A7603036)).
    - **« Émeutes » [riot] exclusion** (L121-8): some files are not covered. The State claim (L211-10 CSI) then becomes a second product ([France Épargne](https://www.france-epargne.fr/news/pillages-du-17-octobre-comment-les-commercants-peuvent-se-faire-indemniser)).
