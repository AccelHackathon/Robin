## 1. Real merchant testimonies

### 1.1 October 2026

- **Pauline, optician in Marseille**: she saved « deux sacs de lunettes » [two bags of glasses] and lost the rest of her stock. She stayed on site for three hours during the clashes and wrote on her shop window that there was « plus rien » [nothing left] to take. She says she invested all her savings in the shop. Quote: « ce moment de guérilla était sans fin » [that moment of guerrilla warfare was endless]. Source: [Sud Radio](https://www.sudradio.fr/sud-radio/il-ny-a-plus-rien-a-marseille-les-commercants-devastes-par-les-pillages-du-17-octobre)
- **Anonymous merchant in Marseille (mobile phones, probably)**: **300 phones stolen**. CCTV shows looters equipped with « marteaux, pieds-de-biche et scies sauteuses thermiques » [hammers, crowbars and petrol-powered jigsaws]. Quote: « Ils savaient ce qu'ils faisaient » [They knew what they were doing]. Source: [Sud Radio](https://www.sudradio.fr/sud-radio/il-ny-a-plus-rien-a-marseille-les-commercants-devastes-par-les-pillages-du-17-octobre)
  - Point of attention for the demo: an account of « équipés » [equipped] and « organisés » [organised] looters is precisely what loses the claim against the State (the « groupe organisé » [organised group] case law, see §4.3).
- **Another Marseille merchant**: brands targeted (Vuitton, Hermès, Rolex) and a gold dealer whose window was 10 cm thick. Quote: « Ils étaient jeunes mais ils étaient équipés. » [They were young but they were equipped.] Source: [Sud Radio](https://www.sudradio.fr/sud-radio/il-ny-a-plus-rien-a-marseille-les-commercants-devastes-par-les-pillages-du-17-octobre)
- **Guillaume Sicard**, president of the Marseille city-centre retailers' association: almost one shop in two looted in the city centre (first estimate). Same source.
- **Thierry Véron**, Paris federation of retailers' associations: « comme d'habitude, les commerçants ne sont pas en première ligne, et c'est eux qui trinquent » [as usual, retailers are not on the front line, yet they are the ones who pay the price]. Same source.
- **Rashadul, shop on boulevard Magenta in Paris**: neighbours and customers opened a crowdfunding pot so he could reopen (according to Franceinfo, quoted by [Le Bonbon](https://lebonbon.fr/paris/news/17-octobre-emmanuel-gregoire-cellule-urgence-commercants/)). Le Bonbon also launched a crowdfunding pot « pour couvrir ce que l'assurance ne couvre pas » [to cover what insurance does not cover] ([Le Bonbon](https://www.lebonbon.fr/paris/news/cagnotte-commercants-pilles-17-octobre/)).
- **Chains affected** ([Public Sénat / AFP](https://www.publicsenat.fr/actualites/economie/pillages-au-moins-3-000-commerces-touchees-pour-des-centaines-de-millions-deuros-de-degats)):
  - BHV Marais: around thirty people forced the entrance and ransacked the ground floor.
  - Galeries Lafayette: Bordeaux, Nantes, Rennes.
  - SFR: around 15 stores.
  - Casino (Monoprix, Franprix): around 50 franchisees.
  - JCDecaux: more than 450 items of street furniture.
  - Carrefour: around 50 stores damaged, of which around 20 looted ([L'Essentiel de l'Éco](https://lessentieldeleco.fr/8991-la-garantie-emeutes-une-loi-votee-pour-rien/)).
  - Apple: stores targeted in Bordeaux and Lille ([search results, unverified](https://www.journaldugeek.com/2026/10/19/manifestation-17-octobre-indemnisations-boutiques-saccagees/)).
- **Michel Picon (U2P)**, after the meeting at Bercy: « rien de concret » [nothing concrete] for now ([Public Sénat](https://www.publicsenat.fr/actualites/economie/pillages-au-moins-3-000-commerces-touchees-pour-des-centaines-de-millions-deuros-de-degats)).
- **Judith Jiguet (FCD)**: « on a besoin aussi d'une accélération des procédures » [we also need procedures to be sped up] for *activité partielle* (short-time work scheme) and compensation ([Bourse Inside](https://bourseinside.fr/?p=13815)).

### 1.2 The 2023 riots (precedent)

- **Yves, deputy manager of a tobacconist's in the Paris region** (ransacked, closed for more than a month): he estimates the damage at **€200,000** and had received only **€55,000** from his insurer. Quote: « Actuellement, on vit en enfer avec ces bagarres avec les assurances. Et même en ayant souscrit au maximum des taux et avec de très bonnes assurances, on attend toujours. » [Right now we are living in hell with these fights with the insurers. And even having taken out the maximum cover with very good insurance, we are still waiting.] Source: Europe 1 ([link](https://www.europe1.fr/node/60897), page blocked with a 403, quote taken from the search engine snippet, **not verified in full**).
  - **This is the best real « hook »: a gap of €145,000 between the estimated loss and what was paid out.**
- **A merchant looted in 2023 waited nearly 5 months for his new shop windows** ([JeChange](https://www.jechange.fr/credit/news/indemnisation-commerces-pilles-parcours-combattant-procedure-stock-plafonne)).
- **Merchants in Denain** left « sans assurance » [without insurance] after the riots of summer 2023 (written question to the Assemblée nationale cited in the search results, [QE](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/16/questions/QANR5L16QG1058.pdf), **unverified**: the PDF has not been read).
- **More than 500 tobacconists' damaged**; flat-rate aid of €10,000 for tobacconists closed for at least 3 days (2023-2027 tobacconists' protocol) ([QE AN 9876](https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/16/questions/QANR5L16QE9876.pdf), search snippet). Senate report n° 521 cites **436 tobacconists and 118 opticians** among around 1,500 shops affected ([Simonnet Avocat](https://www.simonnetavocat.fr/?p=281226)).
- **Merchant cover in 2023**: according to Bruno Le Maire, 90 to 95 % were covered for property damage, but **only 53 % for *perte d'exploitation* (business interruption)** (search snippet on [LSA](https://www.lsa-conso.fr/commerces-pilles-pendant-les-emeutes-voici-la-marche-a-suivre-en-cas-de-degats-et-de-vols,441866), page returns 403, **not verified in full**).

### 1.3 Recurring problems identified in the sources

| Problem | Sourced fact |
|---|---|
| **Stolen stock capped** | Courts often apply the theft cover limit, which is lower than the riot cover limit. Example: TAE Marseille, 3 avril 2025, n° 2024F00787 (Be Weep / GAN). See [JeChange](https://www.jechange.fr/credit/news/indemnisation-commerces-pilles-parcours-combattant-procedure-stock-plafonne) and [Simonnet](https://www.simonnetavocat.fr/?p=281226). |
| **Stock compensated at purchase price** | Stock is compensated at purchase cost, not at selling price: the margin is not paid under the stock heading ([JeChange](https://www.jechange.fr/credit/news/indemnisation-commerces-pilles-parcours-combattant-procedure-stock-plafonne)). |
| ***Franchises* (deductibles)** | In the cases adjudicated: from €455 to nearly €8,000 ([JeChange](https://www.jechange.fr/credit/news/indemnisation-commerces-pilles-parcours-combattant-procedure-stock-plafonne)). Flat amounts « généralement entre 500 et 1 500 € » [generally between €500 and €1,500] according to O. Moustacakis, Assurland ([Bourse Inside](https://bourseinside.fr/?p=13815)). |
| **Business interruption absent or conditional** | Optional cover, triggered only by an insured material damage, with a deductible of several days ([JeChange](https://www.jechange.fr/credit/news/indemnisation-commerces-pilles-parcours-combattant-procedure-stock-plafonne), [Assurup](https://www.assurup.com/blog/articles/emeutes-pillages-indemnisation-commerce-octobre-2026)). |
| **Public aid deducted from business interruption** | A case from the *Médiation de l'Assurance* (insurance ombudsman): the loss adjuster deducts State aid and saved costs, and the indemnity falls to €0 (search snippet, [Médiation PDF](https://www.mediation-assurance.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Etude-de-cas-LMA_2021.12.14_ABR_pertes-exploitation.pdf), unavailable, 503). Conversely, the TAE Marseille (29/01/2026, n° 2024F01593, JCA / SwissLife) ordered the return of **€10,000 wrongly deducted**: the aid under decree 2023-712 is not of the same nature as a business interruption indemnity ([Simonnet](https://www.simonnetavocat.fr/?p=281226)). |
| **Deductible not waived automatically** | In 2026, it must be requested in writing ([JeChange](https://www.jechange.fr/credit/news/indemnisation-commerces-pilles-parcours-combattant-procedure-stock-plafonne)). Exception: Crédit Mutuel (ACM, CIC Assurances) waives deductibles on property damage ([ToutSurMesFinances](https://www.toutsurmesfinances.com/actualites/?p=2532)). |
| **Evidence** | Supplier invoices, accounts, latest inventory. An incomplete file is not processed (JeChange, Assurup). |
| **Time taken for claims against the State** | For the gilets jaunes, 5 to 7 years of proceedings ([JeChange](https://www.jechange.fr/credit/news/indemnisation-commerces-pilles-parcours-combattant-procedure-stock-plafonne), [Simonnet](https://www.simonnetavocat.fr/?p=281226)). |
| **« Faux experts » [fake experts]** | The CCI Paris IDF warns against « experts » who canvass without a mandate (search snippet, [CCI](https://www.entreprises.cci-paris-idf.fr/commercants-sinistres), page returns 503). **To be factored into product positioning: do not look like a door-to-door canvasser.** |

## 2. Specifics of October 2026

### 2.1 The facts
- **Context**: a movement against the cost of living, started in September 2026 by secondary-school students (teacher shortages, overcrowded classes), broadened to fuel, the cost of living and public services ([Reinsurance News](https://www.reinsurancene.ws/france-riots-put-srcc-accumulation-risk-in-focus-for-re-insurers-morningstar-dbrs/)). On Saturday 17/10, there were between 550,000 and 800,000 demonstrators ([L'Essentiel de l'Éco](https://lessentieldeleco.fr/8991-la-garantie-emeutes-une-loi-votee-pour-rien/)). Further clashes took place on Sunday 18/10 in Paris and Lyon ([DH/AFP](https://www.dhnet.be/dernieres-depeches/2026/10/18/la-france-sonnee-au-lendemain-dune-journee-de-mobilisation-ayant-vire-au-chaos-HXF3G7Q5PVEIDLWNLRBQVQW4J4/)).
- **Human and judicial toll**: 2,397 arrests, 997 police officers and gendarmes injured (DH/AFP). Limoges town hall was set on fire.
- **Cities and streets affected**:
  - Paris: place de la République, Strasbourg-Saint-Denis, Champs-Élysées, rue de Rivoli, bd Magenta, Grands Boulevards ([Le Bonbon](https://lebonbon.fr/paris/news/17-octobre-emmanuel-gregoire-cellule-urgence-commercants/), [Economie Matin](https://www.economiematin.fr/?p=234392)).
  - Bordeaux: rue Sainte-Catherine, 50 to 100 shops looted.
  - Marseille: city centre, up to 30,000 looters according to Le Monde (figure considered fragile).
  - Also Lyon, Lille, Toulouse, Rouen, Nice, Nantes, Rennes, Limoges ([Economie Matin](https://www.economiematin.fr/?p=234392), [Simonnet](https://www.simonnetavocat.fr/?p=281226)).
- **Types of businesses**: luxury, electronics and mobile phones (smartphones, computers), branded clothing, food retail (entire aisles destroyed), pharmacies, jewellers, banks ([Economie Matin](https://www.economiematin.fr/?p=234392)), opticians (Sud Radio).
- **Online resale of stolen goods**: Minister Anne Le Hénanff asked platforms to remove listings; Vinted applies a « tolérance zéro » [zero tolerance] policy (Public Sénat).

### 2.2 Public measures
- Interministerial crisis unit on 19/10; Business Council at Bercy on 20/10 with banks and insurers ([Banque des Territoires](https://www.banquedesterritoires.fr/commerces-saccages-le-gouvernement-annonce-des-mesures-durgence-pour-soutenir-les-entreprises)).
- **Deferral of social security and tax deadlines**, **dedicated support fund** (amount and criteria not yet published), ***activité partielle* at 100 %** from 20/10 instead of 60 % of gross pay ([LCP](https://lcp.fr/actualites/saccages-et-pillages-du-17-octobre-l-etat-au-chevet-des-commercants-le-projet-de-loi), [Banque des Territoires](https://www.banquedesterritoires.fr/commerces-saccages-le-gouvernement-annonce-des-mesures-durgence-pour-soutenir-les-entreprises)).
- **PGE** (State-guaranteed loan), priority access to emergency funds, **free loss assessments**, referral to tradespeople ([Economie Matin](https://www.economiematin.fr/?p=234392)).
- **« Casseurs-payeurs » bill** [“rioters pay”]: collective civil liability of participants, possible deductions from family benefits. Examination in the Senate from 27/10 ([LCP](https://lcp.fr/actualites/saccages-et-pillages-du-17-octobre-l-etat-au-chevet-des-commercants-le-projet-de-loi)).
- **Local authorities**:
  - City of Paris: emergency unit with the CCI and the CMA, help with insurance paperwork ([Le Bonbon](https://lebonbon.fr/paris/news/17-octobre-emmanuel-gregoire-cellule-urgence-commercants/)).
  - PACA Region: reactivation of the « solidarité commerces pillés » fund (€10,000 per shop in 2023) ([JeChange](https://www.jechange.fr/credit/news/gilets-jaunes-emeutes-2023-nouvelle-caledonie-facture-casseurs)).
  - Bordeaux: plan worth « plusieurs millions » [several million] ([Economie Matin](https://www.economiematin.fr/?p=234392)).
- **Banks**:
  - Crédit Agricole: moratorium and emergency credit line at 2.5 % up to €20,000 within 48 h ([Public Sénat](https://www.publicsenat.fr/actualites/economie/pillages-au-moins-3-000-commerces-touchees-pour-des-centaines-de-millions-deuros-de-degats)).
  - Caisse d'Épargne and Banque Populaire: emergency loan up to €30,000 ([MoneyVox](https://www.moneyvox.fr/votre-argent/actualites/110886/caisse-epargne-banques-populaires-pret-urgence-jusqua-30-000-euros-apres-les-violences-du-17-octobre), headline only).

### 2.3 Insurers' commitments (France Assureurs press release of 19/10/2026)
- Notification deadline extended to **30 days**; loss assessments organised « sans tarder » [without delay]; sensitive cases handled as a priority; payment of **advances** made easier ([ToutSurMesFinances](https://www.toutsurmesfinances.com/actualites/?p=2532)).
- **Crédit Mutuel (ACM / CIC Assurances)**: deductibles waived on property damage and immediate advance. **Crédit Mutuel and BPCE Assurances**: security guarding covered for up to 72 h; at BPCE, reserved for business customers (same source).
- **Caveat**: the full press release has not been read. Theft must still be notified as quickly as possible together with the *plainte* (police complaint) ([JeChange](https://www.jechange.fr/credit/news/indemnisation-commerces-pilles-parcours-combattant-procedure-stock-plafonne)).
- **Premiums**: increase of +20 to +50 % being considered for exposed city-centre shops, with possible new exclusions ([Economie Matin](https://www.economiematin.fr/?p=234392), **unverified**, no primary source).

## 3. What policyholder's loss assessors recover

### 3.1 Uplift figures (all from firms or commercial websites: to be presented as « revendiqués par la profession » [claimed by the profession])
| Source | Claimed uplift versus the initial offer | Fees |
|---|---|---|
| [ma-contre-expertise.fr](https://ma-contre-expertise.fr/) | +15 to +30 % | 5-10 % of the indemnity; 8-10 % between €15,000 and €30,000; 6-8 % between €30,000 and €100,000; 5-6 % above €100,000 |
| [Fire Forensic](https://fire-forensic.com/combien-coute-expert-assure-tarifs-rentabilite/) | +35 to +55 % (fire); general range « 30 à 80 % » [30 to 80 %] | 5-12 %; flat fee from €800 to €3,000; €100-200 excl. VAT/hour |
| Directories and guides (aggregated by the search) | +15 to +40 % for a business with a major loss (example: +28 %) | 3-8 % on large business cases |
| [ToutSurMesFinances](https://www.toutsurmesfinances.com/assurance/expert-assure-assurance-habitation.html) | — | 5-10 % of the indemnity, sometimes calculated on the uplift alone; up to 12-18 % on small water-damage claims |

- **Break-even threshold**: a loss assessor's involvement is said to pay off mainly above €10,000 of loss ([Fire Forensic](https://fire-forensic.com/combien-coute-expert-assure-tarifs-rentabilite/)). The fees are a tax-deductible expense for a business.
- **Unregulated profession**: policyholder loss assessment is « encadrée par aucune disposition légale » [governed by no legal provision] according to a parliamentary amendment ([AN](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/16/amendements/1022/AN/77.pdf), from the snippet). **Product argument: transparency and traceability.**
- **« Honoraires d'expert » cover** [loss assessor's fees cover]: it exists in some policies, often capped ([Luko](https://fr.luko.eu/conseils/guide/quest-ce-que-la-garantie-honoraires-dexpert-dassure/)). Without it, the costs stay with the policyholder ([Assurup](https://www.assurup.com/blog/articles/emeutes-pillages-indemnisation-commerce-octobre-2026)). A recent decision refused to reimburse the fees of a policyholder who did not prove the loss assessor was necessary ([search snippet, CA Agen 22/07/2026](https://www.doctrine.fr/d/CA/Agen/2026/CAP160F6D9D65E9A7603036), **unverified**). By contrast, the CAA Paris (administrative court of appeal) (13/03/2026, n° 24PA03813) awarded €2,610 in loss assessor's fees against the State ([Kohen Avocats](https://kohenavocats.fr/?p=2151189)).
- The firms named in the brief (D&R Experts, Cotranex, Union des Experts…) **have no public figures that could be found**. Nothing is invented about them.

### 3.2 Items often forgotten or undervalued (sourced synthesis)
1. ***Perte d'exploitation* (business interruption), and above all its basis of calculation**: it is the *marge brute assurée* (insured gross margin) as defined by the policy, not turnover ([Assurup](https://www.assurup.com/blog/articles/emeutes-pillages-indemnisation-commerce-octobre-2026)). A misestimated margin « peut avoir de sérieuses incidences » [can have serious consequences] ([Legalplace](https://www.legalplace.fr/guides/assurance-pertes-exploitation/)). Growth and seasonality must be taken into account: October-December is the Christmas season.
2. **Additional operating costs**: equipment hire, temporary premises, customer communication, subcontracting ([Generali](https://www.generali.fr/professionnel/dossier/perte-dexploitation-entreprise-multirisques/), [Keobiz](https://keobiz.fr/le-mag/lassurance-perte-dexploitation/)). They are admitted if they helped limit the fall in turnover ([UT Capitole thesis](https://publications.ut-capitole.fr/19769/1/19769_Krajeski.pdf)).
3. **Public aid wrongly deducted from business interruption**: €10,000 returned by the TAE Marseille, JCA / SwissLife ([Simonnet](https://www.simonnetavocat.fr/?p=281226)).
4. **Protective measures**: security guarding, temporary boarding-up, clearance. They are admitted by administrative case law ([Simonnet](https://www.simonnetavocat.fr/?p=281226)) and covered for 72 h at Crédit Mutuel and BPCE.
5. **Excessive *vétusté* (depreciation)**: example of a 15-point reduction obtained on furniture ([Fire Forensic](https://fire-forensic.com/combien-coute-expert-assure-tarifs-rentabilite/)). The ***valeur à neuf* (replacement-as-new) top-up** is paid only against a replacement invoice, often within 2 years, and often capped at 25-30 % ([JeChange](https://www.jechange.fr/assurance/habitation/news/valeur-a-neuf-sinistre-mobilier-vetuste)). See Cass. 2e civ., 7 juil. 2022, n° 20-19.264.
6. **Tenant's improvements and fittings**: they are attached to « contenu » [contents] in some policies (example of a 2006 Belgian policy, [P&V](https://pv.be/documents/1465818/1640754/Conditions+G%C3%A9n%C3%A9rales+%27Multirisk%27+-+2006/2a903f43-8e2d-432c-9023-a9cacef64d4f)). The French rule has not been verified: the policy must be checked.
7. **Stock value**: purchase cost, or **replacement value** when higher (supplier inflation), depending on the policy ([Finalib](https://finalib.fr/blog/credit-financement/assurance-stock-marchandises-entreprise)). Selling price is used only if a specific clause provides for it.
8. **The theft limit applied instead of the riot limit**: this is a key point of discussion (TAE Marseille, Be Weep / GAN). The ***conditions particulières* (policy schedule) prevail over the *conditions générales* (general conditions)** (Cass. 2e civ., 23 mars 2017, n° 16-10.589).
9. **The balance not compensated by the insurer** (deductible, depreciation, limits, uninsured business interruption) **can be claimed from the State** on the basis of art. L211-10 of the CSI. The subrogated insurer recovers only what it paid ([Simonnet](https://www.simonnetavocat.fr/?p=281226)). **This is an item the merchant almost always forgets.**
10. **Over-declaration**: declaring selling price or non-existent goods exposes the policyholder to *déchéance* (forfeiture) for misrepresentation (standard clause, **unverified** against a specific policy). The loss assessor also protects against this risk.

### 4.3 State liability (art. L211-10 CSI): the complementary route
- **CAA Paris, 26 avr. 2024, n° 22PA05252** (Axa / « Max et Moi », looting on 8/12/2018 at 4:38 pm): €80,613 awarded, including €78,089 of goods. **It is for the State to prove that it was a « groupe organisé » [organised group].** ([Simonnet](https://www.simonnetavocat.fr/?p=281226))
- **CAA Paris, 13 mars 2026, n° 24PA03872** (Louis Roederer): State held liable without witness or camera, on the basis of a body of timestamps; €12,000 deductible awarded. **CAA Paris, 13 mars 2026, n° 24PA03813**: €40,899.88 and €2,610 in loss assessor's fees, with no police complaint filed ([Kohen](https://kohenavocats.fr/?p=2151189)).
- **CAA Paris, 13 mai 2026, n° 25PA00588** (JCDecaux): €811,161.90 awarded out of around €1.72 M claimed. Police complaints must be precise; social media videos are not enough.
- **CAA Paris, 12 mai 2022, n° 21PA04931**: deductible of €452.88 reimbursed; the claim for depreciation was rejected for lack of a direct link ([Kohen](https://kohenavocats.fr/?p=2151189)).
- **CE, 28 févr. 2025, n° 473904** (Lib Industries): the test is the **main purpose** of the action (expressing discontent), not premeditation.
- **Unfavourable (« groupe organisé » or pure looting)**:
  - CE, 11 oct. 2023, n° 465591;
  - CE, 6 févr. 2026, n° 494653 (Martinique, €300,000 refused);
  - CE, 18 mars 2026, n° 496675 et 496688;
  - CAA Paris, 3 juil. 2026, n° 26PA00914 (New Caledonia: fault of the State found, but causal link not established).
- **For the demo**: describe the facts (time, street, number of people, link with the march) **without characterising them**. Ban « casseurs organisés » [organised rioters], « venus exprès » [came on purpose] and « émeutiers » [rioters] from correspondence ([Simonnet](https://www.simonnetavocat.fr/?p=281226)). **An AI agent that rereads the notification and the police complaint to remove these words is a concrete, defensible feature.**
- **Limitation period against the State**: four years, until **31/12/2030** for the facts of 17/10/2026; a quantified prior claim to the prefect is mandatory (R421-1 CJA).

### 4.4 Exclusion clauses (L113-1, « formelle et limitée » [formal and limited])
- **Cass. 1re civ., 22 mai 2001, n° 99-10.849**: a clause that needs to be interpreted is not formal and limited ([Le Monde du Droit](https://www.lemondedudroit.fr/secteurs/287-assurances/67950-la-clause-dexclusion-de-garantie-doit-etre-formelle-et-limitee.html), via the snippet).
- **Pourvoi n° 18-14.817 (2019)**: breach of L113-1, because the clause was open to interpretation (same source, **unverified**).
- **Cass. 2e civ., 3 avr. 2025, n° 23-20.003**: exclusion of theft by a person living in the household ([Eurojuris](https://www.eurojuris.fr/page/articles/exclusion-garantie-vol-commis-par-personne-vivant-au-foyer-de-l-assure-43328.htm)).
- **Cass., 17 sept. 2026, n° 25-12.441** (brasserie, Covid): the formal character is assessed **exclusion by exclusion**; a vague exclusion is set aside without bringing down the whole clause, unless it affects its intelligibility ([Selectra](https://selectra.info/assurance/actualites/marche/clause-exclusion-floue-assurance-cassation), **unverified**, chamber not specified).

### 4.5 Interpretation in favour of the policyholder
- **Art. 1190 C. civ.**: in case of doubt, a contract of adhesion is interpreted against the party who proposed it. **CA Rennes, 29 avr. 2026, n° 23/02010**: ambiguity benefits the business policyholder, even where a *courtier* (broker) was involved, as long as the general conditions were not negotiated ([Kohen](https://kohenavocats.com/cour-dappel-de-rennes-le-29-avril-2026-n23-02010/)).
- **Art. L211-1 C. conso.** (formerly L133-2): interpretation most favourable to the consumer. **Does not apply to a merchant acting for business purposes**, hence the relevance of art. 1190 (own analysis, **unverified** by any decision).
- No Cour de cassation ruling applying art. 1190 to a business policy was found.

### 4.6 Late notification
- **Art. L113-2 al. final**: forfeiture for late notification can be invoked only if the insurer proves that the delay caused it a loss (except in cases of fortuitous event or force majeure).
- **Cass. 2e civ., 21 janv. 2021, n° 19-13.347**: a clause setting a 4-day deadline, i.e. below the legal minimum, is unenforceable; forfeiture requires a valid clause and a loss ([Lextenso](https://www.labase-lextenso.fr/l-essentiel-droit-des-assurances/2021-n3/sanction-de-la-clause-enserrant-la-declaration-de-sinistre-dans-un-delai-inferieur-au-minimum-legal-DAS113g7), [Kohen](https://kohenavocats.fr/?p=2134839)).
- **Cass. 2e civ., 4 juil. 2019, n° 18-18.444**: forfeiture must be provided for in the policy ([Le Monde du Droit](https://www.lemondedudroit.fr/secteurs/287-assurances/66406-declaration-tardive-la-decheance-de-garantie-doit-etre-prevue-au-contrat.html)).
- **Cass. 2e civ., 7 mai 2009, n° 08-11.391**: no established loss, no forfeiture ([ANIL](https://www.anil.org/documentation-experte/analyses-juridiques-jurisprudence/jurisprudence/jurisprudence-2009/assurance-emprunteur-/declaration-tardive-du-sinistre/-consequences/)).
- **Cass. 3e civ., 9 avr. 2026, n° 24-12.608**: the loss must be concrete; a generic « risque accru » [increased risk] is not enough ([Kohen](https://kohenavocats.fr/?p=2151189), **unverified**).

### 4.7 Enforceability of unsigned general conditions
- **Cass. 2e civ., 14 juin 2018, n° 17-19.717**: signing the policy schedule, which refers to the special conditions and acknowledges their receipt, makes them enforceable ([BJDA](https://bjda.fr/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/COMM_CA_2_n58.pdf)). It is for the insurer to prove delivery.
- **Cass. 7 nov. 2024, n° 23-10.612** and **Cass. 7 mai 2026, n° 24-12.952**: clarifications on proof of delivery ([Le Monde du Droit](https://www.lemondedudroit.fr/secteurs/287-assurances/104659-effets-de-l-absence-de-signature-des-cp-par-l-assure.html), **unverified**).
- **For the demo**: the agent asks the insurer for a copy of the **signed** general conditions and policy schedule, together with proof of their delivery, before accepting an exclusion.

### 4.8 Two-year limitation period (L114-1)
- **Art. R112-1**: the policy must state the limitation period, its grounds for interruption (L114-2) and its starting point, **failing which the 2-year period is unenforceable**.
- Case law: Cass. 1re civ., 29 juin 2016, n° 15-19.751; Cass. 2e civ., 9 févr. 2023, n° 21-19.498; Cass. 2e civ., 12 févr. 2026, n° 24-18.683, which requires the statement to appear in the endorsement ([Lextenso](https://www.labase-lextenso.fr/jp-commente/cc/2016-06/15-19-751-CC-29062016-15_19751), [BJDA](https://bjda.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/12Lpqad45PNbv4_CA_4.pdf)).
- For the facts of 17/10/2026: 2 years against the insurer (unless unenforceable), 4 years against the State ([Simonnet](https://www.simonnetavocat.fr/?p=281226)).

**Adversarial loss assessment and *tiers expert* (umpire / third expert)**: there is no general « droit à la contre-expertise » [right to a counter-appraisal] written into the Code des assurances for damage to business property. The right comes from:
- (a) the policy: the « expertise / désaccord » [assessment / disagreement] clause of the general conditions usually provides that each party appoints its own loss assessor and that, in case of disagreement, a third expert is appointed, failing agreement by the president of the court. This is **standard practice, not verified against a model policy**;
- (b) the interim application for expert assessment under art. 145 CPC ([Doctrine snippet](https://www.doctrine.fr/d/TJ/Paris/2025/TJP586E2EB728C6E1992AE6));
- (c) the ever-open possibility of appointing one's own loss assessor ([JeChange](https://www.jechange.fr/credit/news/indemnisation-commerces-pilles-parcours-combattant-procedure-stock-plafonne), [Assurup](https://www.assurup.com/blog/articles/emeutes-pillages-indemnisation-commerce-octobre-2026)).

Amicable loss assessment « peut être imposée par une clause du contrat » [may be imposed by a clause of the policy] ([AP-HP Affaires juridiques](https://affairesjuridiques.aphp.fr/textes/lexpertise-apres-sinistre/?pdf=605402)). Remedies thereafter: complaints department, then **Médiation de l'Assurance**, then the courts.

- **Typical initial offer from the insurer (scenario)**: stock brought down to the €15,000 theft limit, plus window €3,600, plus shutter and till after 30 % depreciation (€3,150), less a €1,000 deductible, i.e. **≈ €20,750**.
- **Loss assessor's levers**:

  2. Have the business interruption quantified on gross margin with N-1 and N-2 comparatives.

### Case C: « Optique Pauline, looted stock, Marseille »
**Real anchor**: the Marseille optician who saved « deux sacs de lunettes » [two bags of glasses] ([Sud Radio](https://www.sudradio.fr/sud-radio/il-ny-a-plus-rien-a-marseille-les-commercants-devastes-par-les-pillages-du-17-octobre)); 118 opticians affected in 2023 ([Simonnet](https://www.simonnetavocat.fr/?p=281226)); an optician's stock of €50,000 to €200,000 ([Hayot Expertise](https://hayot-expertise.fr/secteurs/expert-comptable-opticien)); gross margin on frames of 58 % (same source, via the snippet).

**Facts**: the shop is looted for 3 hours on Saturday afternoon, while the owner is present. Around 90 % of the stock of frames and sunglasses is stolen; display units and the shop window are destroyed; customers' finished glasses ready for collection are stolen. The shop is closed for 6 weeks, the time needed to restock from suppliers.

- **Pitfalls specific to this case**:
  1. **Theft limit** on stock (TAE Marseille, Be Weep / GAN).
  2. A **standard clause** excludes or reduces theft if the protective devices were not in operation: the shop was open, with the shutter up. **Unverified** against a model policy, but to be anticipated. **Counter-argument**: this is looting during a riot, so the riot extension should apply, and any exclusion must be formal and limited (L113-1).
  3. **Over-valuation at selling price** exposes the policyholder to forfeiture.
  4. Do not write « pilleurs organisés » [organised looters] in the police complaint (the Sud Radio account of « équipés » [equipped] looters is precisely what would lose the claim against the State).
- **Gap**: between the capped offer (≈ €25,000, assuming a €20,000 theft limit plus window and shutter, less the deductible) and the defensible file (≈ €110,000-120,000 with the State as a top-up), i.e. **≈ €85,000-95,000**.

## 7. Key takeaways for the pitch
1. **The problem is real and dated**: at least 3,000 shops affected (Prime Minister), €1 to 1.2 bn of insured losses (Morningstar DBRS), 70 % small retailers (FCD).
2. **The gap is documented**: a tobacconist with €200,000 of damage had received only €55,000 in 2023 (Europe 1); loss assessors claim +15 to +40 %.
3. **The window for action is short**: 30 days to notify (France Assureurs); 2 business days is the legal minimum for theft.
4. **The public regime offers no protection**: the LFI 2026 riot cover is passed but not in force.
5. **The balance not paid by the insurer can be claimed from the State** (L211-10 CSI, before 31/12/2030), but the words used in the police complaint can lose everything. An agent that rereads and rephrases is a concrete feature.
